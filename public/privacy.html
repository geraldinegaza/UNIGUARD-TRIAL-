Privacy Notice

Issued under Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The Personal Information Controller is [Municipal Government of Lingayen, through the Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office]. Its Data Protection Officer can be reached at [dpo@example.gov.ph].

2. What we collect

  • Account details: full name, email address, mobile number and barangay.
  • Hazard reports: the hazard type, your description, the date and time, the coordinates recorded by your device, and any photo you attach.
  • Device data needed to alert you: a push subscription endpoint, and the browser identifier your device supplies.
  • Activity records: actions taken by officials and LGU staff, kept in an audit log.

3. Why we collect it

  • To receive, verify and act on hazard reports in your barangay.
  • To send you official advisories and emergency alerts.
  • To coordinate the response and account for how each report was handled.
  • To publish statistics that cannot identify any individual.

We rely on your consent, and on the performance of our public mandate for disaster risk reduction and management. You may withdraw consent at any time, but an account without consent cannot file reports or receive alerts.

4. Who can see your report

Your name is visible to the barangay officials of your barangay and to LGU / LDRRMC personnel, because they act on it. Other residents can see that a hazard was reported and where, through a feed that carries no reporter identity at all. Statistics are published only in aggregate.

5. How long we keep it

Hazard reports and their photos are kept for [five years] from the date the incident is closed, then deleted. Audit records are kept for [the retention period your records office requires]. Account data is deleted when you close your account, or after [twenty-four months] of inactivity.

6. Where it is stored

Data is stored with our managed database and file storage provider, [Supabase / your chosen host and region]. Access is limited by role based rules enforced in the database itself, not by interface controls. All traffic is encrypted in transit.

7. Your rights

  • To be informed that your data is being processed.
  • To access, correct and object to the processing of your data.
  • To erasure or blocking, subject to our legal retention duties.
  • To data portability, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Write to the Data Protection Officer at [dpo@example.gov.ph] to exercise any of these rights. We answer within fifteen working days.

8. In an emergency

If you are in danger, do not wait for an acknowledgement in the application. Call your barangay desk or the city DRRMO on the hotline listed in the application.
